Proprietary Intelligence Products

Three Indexes. No Comparable Data Exists.

Built from first principles, grounded in CIPC data, banking disclosures, and forensic analysis of active proceedings. Published weekly. Calibrated over time. Never hedged.

SADI
South Africa Distress Index
A composite weekly score tracking six dimensions of systemic corporate distress: business rescue filing volume, liquidation volume, aggregate rescue probability inverse, banking NPL trend, retrenchment volume, and SOE distress. Scale: 0 (benign) to 100 (systemic crisis). Published every Monday with full methodology disclosed to Premium subscribers.

RPS
Rescue Probability Score
A company-level rescue success probability for every active major rescue proceeding. Eight-factor weighted model: leverage, liquidity, sector fragility, labour risk, creditor alignment, management quality, legal risk, and macro environment. Scored 0–100% with categories from STRONG (80%+) to TERMINAL (below 10%). Updated weekly.

RVI
Rescue Velocity Index
Pipeline health metric measuring the rate at which rescue cases are resolving against the rate at which new cases are entering the system. RVI above 1.0: system clearing. RVI below 1.0: pipeline building. RVI below 0.5: capacity risk. Proprietary leading indicator of systemic BRP and court capacity stress.
