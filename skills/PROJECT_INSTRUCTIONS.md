## FOUNDATIONAL POLICY: VERIFIED-ONLY CONTENT

**This policy is absolute. It governs every agent, every session, every edition, without exception.**

From Edition 008 onwards, no hypothetical, illustrative, simulated, invented, or estimated company-specific content will appear in any output of this system.

### Why this policy exists
The BR Distress Ledger's value is its credibility. Editions 001–007 experienced a cycle of invented content → corrections → credibility erosion → further corrections. The cause was a practice of inserting "illustrative" or "simulated" company case studies when real CIPC data was not available. However well-labelled, this content was indistinguishable to time-pressed readers from real reporting. It forced repeated correction notices, damaged reader trust, and created legal and reputational risk.

The solution is structural: when real verified data is not available, this publication produces a **Data Gap Notice** — a professional, transparent placeholder that tells the reader what data is missing, where to find it, and what verified context is available in the meantime. A publication with Data Gap Notices is more credible than one with invented tables.

### What is prohibited
- ❌ Inventing company names, registration numbers, or filing details
- ❌ Simulating BRP appointments, PCF transactions, or creditor stances at named companies
- ❌ Creating fictional court cases or attributing holdings to cases that don't exist
- ❌ Assigning RPS scores to invented companies
- ❌ Running "simulated case study threads" across editions
- ❌ Labelling invented content as "illustrative" and treating it as acceptable
- ❌ Carrying prior-edition data forward without re-verifying it this cycle

### What replaces it
- ✅ Verified company data supplied by the publisher from CIPC/Gazette records
- ✅ Verified macro intelligence from primary sources (SARB, Stats SA, DMPR, Eskom, etc.)
- ✅ Verified legal intelligence from SAFLII and named law firm alerts
- ✅ Analytical inference from verified inputs, clearly labelled as `[ANALYTICAL]`
- ✅ Data Gap Notices when required data is not available
- ✅ Aggregate sector/macro commentary that does not require company-specific data

## PROPRIETARY INTELLIGENCE PRODUCTS

Three proprietary metrics appear in every full edition:

### SADI — South Africa Distress Index
Scale: 0–100. Components: Filing volume (25%) + Liquidation volume (20%) + Aggregate RPS inverse (20%) + NPL trend (15%) + Retrenchment volume (10%) + SOE distress (10%).
Sub-components drawn from verified inputs where available; labelled `[ANALYTICAL ESTIMATE]` where verification is pending.

### RPS — Rescue Probability Score
Produced only for real companies supplied by the publisher from CIPC/SENS records.
Eight-factor weighted model: Leverage (20%) + Liquidity (20%) + Sector (15%) + Creditor alignment (15%) + Labour (10%) + Management (10%) + Legal (5%) + Macro (5%).
Categories: STRONG / PROBABLE / CHALLENGED / DISTRESSED / CRITICAL / TERMINAL.

### RVI — Rescue Velocity Index
Measures verified case resolution rate vs verified intake rate per week.
Calculated from publisher-supplied CIPC data. Data Gap Notice if CIPC data not supplied.
